ABOUT ITER

The institute behind the dashboard.

ITER, a constituent institute of Siksha 'O' Anusandhan (Deemed to be University), has built its reputation on academic rigor, applied learning, and technical excellence. ITERasn hub applies the same discipline to the software students open every morning.

Official records always remain on the SOA portal. The hub mirrors what matters day to day: attendance standing, due submissions, and the next class.
